Conclusion: checkCredentials is a `view` with an explicit atTime argument; a witness obtains the
historical decision by eth_call at the decision's PBC block AND atTime = that block's timestamp.
List generation/versions come from the block's state; atTime only drives the time comparisons. This
needs a node that serves historical state (Anvil does by default; a pruned node may not).
