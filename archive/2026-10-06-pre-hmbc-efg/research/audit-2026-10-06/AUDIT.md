# Source, claim and specification audit — 2026-10-06

**Verdict: the plans cannot be certified as factually complete or implementation-ready.** The base papers are identifiable and several important source observations hold. However, the archive contains incorrect readings of the threat model, unproved repair guarantees, incompatible planning defaults and unanswered specification questions. Resolving a citation identifier does not verify the claim attributed to it.

This audit corrects the active plans and records remaining uncertainty. Historical reports and communications are preserved as evidence of what earlier sessions said, not endorsed as correct. Baseline inspected: Git commit `7188d4854b5747bf04084745ea4828449b36bfc1`.

IACR links, software documentation, institutional pages, patents and references named without an identifier are not certified by the DOI/arXiv sweep. ## Citation and specification release gates

1. For every statement used in the final paper, select an exact primary source/version and record its passage, supported scope and caveats. Import authoritative metadata from the register; do not paste historical “OK” labels into a bibliography. Read the closed-access texts before relying on their mechanisms or their non-overlap with the project. All prior “O” annotations that do not distinguish metadata/abstract/full text remain insufficient claim evidence.
2. Complete `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`. It is legitimate to propose G=1, epsilon 2, t=1, public answers, particular validators or k attesters, but these must be labeled choices and turned into explicit specifications. They are not conclusions of HMBC2020.
3. For E, pin versions and define the actual attacker/call paths before reproducing findings. For a paper-derived Solidity reimplementation, list every choice filling an original gap and report counterexamples against that constructed model accordingly.
4. For A, resolve setup/transcript leakage, complete query and sensitivity definitions, and prove accounting with the actual distributed sampler and observable protocol. For G, define ciphertext/key lifecycle before claiming secrecy. For D, specify attesters' evidence, independence, time and randomness before proving agreement.
5. Recheck venue deadlines and scope on official pages when submitting. Do not promise acceptance, firstness, complete literature coverage or a secrecy guarantee based on search snippets, numerical confidence estimates or repeated AI reports.

**Remaining limitation:** this audit does not certify all 345 works' full contents or every historical factual statement. Those are explicitly unresolved, rather than silently treated as correct. The active documents have been corrected at the identified points and now make their remaining specifications/proof obligations visible.
