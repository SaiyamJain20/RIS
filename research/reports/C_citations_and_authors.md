## Part 2a - Malamas PhD thesis (OPENED: full PDF text read for TOC, structure, conclusions, future work)
Source: Univ. of Piraeus repository, https://dione.lib.unipi.gr/xmlui/handle/unipi/16570 ; PDF .../bitstream/handle/unipi/16570/Malamas_PLD1804.pdf ; 148 pp, June 2024, advisor P. Kotzanikolaou.
Title: "Distributed security and trust management in multi-authority and multi-domain environments based on blockchain. Case studies in healthcare and supply chain management systems."
Abstract (verbatim fragment): "...proposes a hierarchical multi-blockchain solution (HMBAC) with implementations through the Janus framework... Additional security features include Self Sovereign Identity infrastructure for device authentication and legacy ERP system integration."
Chapters: 1 Intro; 2 Literature review; 3 HMBAC model; 4 System design; 5 Implementation (Frontend API, Inter-Blockchain API, contracts, distributed ABE decryption, isolation); 6 Security + performance analysis (attack tree, benchmarks); 7 Distributed SSI management (IoMT device auth with DIDs/VCs, ZKP selective disclosure, revocation notifications); 8 Legacy ERP interconnection (supply chain); 9 Conclusions and future work.
Published-paper basis (thesis refs [1]-[10]): forensics-by-design framework 2019; IoMT risk assessment survey (IEEE Access 2021); supply chain risk assessment (CyMaEn 2023); Janus (2023); HMBC2020; IoMT device authN (ECICE 2020, Fotopoulos et al.); Uninterrupted Trust (SEEDA 2023); ERP service layer (CoDIT 2023); Lightning network review (2023); green bonds (2023).
Grep of the full text for post-quantum / quantum / differential privacy / MPC / break-glass / consent (patient) / firmware transparency / unlinkability / aggregate or proxy signatures / BFT returns NOTHING as a contribution (only: ZKP in SSI ch.7, homomorphic encryption in related work "impractical", thresholds = CA-admin voting).
THESIS FUTURE WORK (Sec. 9.2, verbatim list): (1) more use cases; (2) "Exploring advanced cryptographic techniques and integrating machine learning algorithms for predictive access control"; (3) socio-economic impacts; (4) "Hardware and Software Trusted Computing (Hardware Immutability)" - trusted hardware/secure boot/HSM; (5) SSI in the Metaverse (AR/VR, homomorphic enc., DIDs); (6) "Interoperability with Different Blockchains ... cross-chain communication protocols, atomic swaps, and blockchain bridges". Also: "integration of additional privacy-preserving mechanisms" listed as a limitation.
Sec. 4.5 of the thesis again lists as examples: manufacturers push signed firmware, "insurance companies [access] statistical hospital treatment data ... will be able to prove that the privacy of personal health records is preserved" - claimed only via PBC logging, no technical mechanism (supports gaps g, h, i).

CORRECTION to Part 1b-bis: "Self-Sovereign Identity in Semi-Permissioned Blockchain Networks Leveraging Ethereum and Hyperledger Fabric" (ICDH 2023) is NOT the Piraeus group (authors Sahi, Liang, Van Devanter, Oikonomou, Zhang; patient SSI on Fabric with Keccak CA) - a passing cite of JANUS. The SAME-GROUP citers of JANUS are the SEEDA 2023 and CoDIT 2023 papers (both supply chain) plus the 2024 Metaverse survey and CoDIT 2025 AGV paper.
