## 0. Facts re-verified in hmbc2020.txt
- III-C4 (l.555-590): Rule 2 = `IF a.isDoctorAtHospital(*) AND a.onDuty(*) THEN X.allow(a, MHR(b)), forall MHR(b) in DB_X`. The formal text has NO emergency/incident predicate; only the prose says "patients under emergency treatment". I-B2 (l.150-153) states the INTENT as patient-scoped ("temporarily full access to the medical history of a particular patient ... currently treating this patient in an A&E"); the mechanism is attribute-scoped.
- III-C6 (l.647-651): "Each hospital X is responsible to independently update, sign and publish TempACL_X"; entry = (GID, temporal role, period). No patient id, no incident id, no second signer.
- III-E2 (l.878-885): KSSC partially decrypts with K_{GID,onDuty(X)}; user finishes with long-term key.
- V-A5 (l.1249-1270): "forensics" = global log + "globally detectable policy modification" + collusion resistance "provided that a majority of stakeholders are honest". Nothing preventive about emergency use; nobody analyses the log.
- I-B rejects patient manual approval in emergencies. So any fix must be non-blocking for the clinician (no human approval on the critical path).

## 1. Candidates (sharpened)
**C-I  CASE-SCOPED EMERGENCY TICKETS (prevent).** Replace "onDuty = blanket" by an *Emergency Ticket* tau = (ticket_id, GID_clinician, patient pseudonym/admission id, [t0,t1], reason code, quota class) filed on the PBC through a new Emergency Smart Contract (ESC). Release requires >= k signed attestations from k *distinct, independent* authorities (machine attesters, no human on the critical path: e.g. clinician's home hospital confirms on-duty-at-ED, patient's data-owning hospital confirms ED admission / no protective flag, a random third stakeholder checks quota). k attesters are drawn per ticket by on-chain verifiable randomness from the TMSC trust-anchor set. The ticket is a self-authenticating capability (k signatures verifiable against trust anchors), so the DBC's ACSC can verify it WITHOUT trusting the Inter-BC API middleware (closes part of seed gap e). ESC then (a) emits a patient/next-of-kin notification event to the patient's home authority, (b) records a time-boxed review obligation (random independent reviewer, deadline Delta, escalation through the existing majorityConsent), (c) increments per-GID quota. 2020 Rule 2 becomes `... AND validTicket(a, b, now)`. No threshold of the KSSC/gatekeeper key (exclusion zone 2 respected); plain k ECDSA/Ed25519 signatures, no new crypto. Fail-safe mode for liveness (degraded ticket, k'=1, lower quota, mandatory review) is part of the safety/liveness trade-off analysis.
**C-II INSIDER-MISUSE DETECTION on the global log (detect).** Two layers: (1) deterministic reconciliation - every PSC-forwarded emergency request must match a ticket (release-without-ticket = violation, no ML); (2) statistical/graph flagging of *ticketed* accesses (volume, clinician-patient graph distance, attester-pair correlation, off-ED hours, VIP/co-worker/neighbour snooping features) with federated/threshold-opened escalation. PLUS security of the detector: (a) mimicry/community-camouflage by a colluding insider clique, (b) poisoning of the baseline by colluding attesters/authorities (<k and >=k), (c) effect of a minority of PBC nodes tampering with/omitting log entries (Merkle-anchor completeness check).
**C-III BENCHMARK REALISM.** Synthea encounters -> role-aware access-log generator, partitioned over 3-4 domains, labelled insider scenarios (snoop, VIP, bulk, credential misuse, rogue CA-Admin TempACL, attester collusion), calibrated to published statistics.

## 5. PoC feasibility (3 laptops: ~14 GB, 16 threads, CPU only)
- Stack: Python 3 simulation of PBC/DBC (hash-chained ledger + Ed25519/ECDSA via `cryptography`), optional Solidity ESC on ganache/hardhat (original authors used ganache-cli; no Fabric/K8s); ProVerif (OCaml, light) or Scyther for the ticket protocol; Synthea (JVM, cap -Xmx2g, ~5-20k patients) for encounters; scikit-learn (Isolation Forest, LR, community deviation) for detectors. No GPU.
- Person-weeks (3 people, ~3-4 calendar weeks): protocol + ProVerif 2 pw; ESC/ACSC integration + latency/gas 2 pw; log generator + 6 scenario families 2 pw; detectors + poisoning/mimicry experiments 2 pw; writing/figures 2 pw => ~10 pw.
- Pitfalls: Synthea has no staff/access model (must build assignment of clinicians to encounters); circularity (detector finds what generator injects) - hold out scenario families, report exposure/time-to-detect not only AUC; keep RSS < 3.5 GB per process (laptop OOM history), run Synthea once and cache; do not run Fabric; k-of-n real-time latency measured on LAN only (state that WAN differs); notification to abusers/partners is a real-world harm (discuss, make recipient policy-configurable).

## 6. Evaluation plan
- Threat model: A1 licensed clinician snooping; A2 rogue CA-Admin minting TempACL (code-verified in JANUS TMSC; 2020 text allows it); A3 f of N authorities captured/colluding (small-domain capture, links to GC4); A4 compromised domain majority ignoring tickets (ACSC/KSSC); A5 ticket replay/relay/cross-patient reuse; A6 mimicry/camouflage clique vs detector; A7 colluding attesters poisoning baselines; A8 minority PBC nodes tampering/omitting log entries.
- Formal: ProVerif correspondence queries - (a) release(C,P,t) => ticket(C,P,w) with t in w and k distinct attestations (injective); (b) ticket for P cannot unlock P'; (c) replay/expiry; (d) release => notification event. Game-based bound: forging a ticket needs EUF-CMA break or >=k attesters controlled; with random committee draw Pr[adversary controls all k of n_s drawn] = hypergeometric C(f,k)/C(N,k) (state exactly, then validate by Monte Carlo). Honest limit: attestations certify plausibility, not truth of the emergency; the guarantee is bounded blast radius + non-silent + reviewable, not "no abuse".
- Metrics: security - exposure E(f,k,N,quota) patients reachable vs 2020 baseline (= all patients in domain); undetected-exposure x time-to-detect; TPR@1%FPR, reviewer alerts/day; AUC/recall drop vs poisoning fraction and clique size; performance - emergency-path latency (ticket->release) vs the paper's 603 ms @100 concurrent, tx/gas per ticket, ticket size, k-vs-latency curve, liveness under attester outage (fail-closed vs degraded).
- Baselines: 2020 onDuty rule; JANUS shift-calendar rule; Al Amin-style 2-of-2 intra-org multisig; EACMS patient-set rule; detectors: Boxwala-style LR, CADS-like community deviation, Isolation Forest, Fabbri-style single-domain explanation templates.

## 8. Kill criteria and likely reviewer objections
Kill if: (1) a 2024-26 paper with cross-domain/multi-authority co-attested break-glass is found (re-check IEEE Xplore/ACM DL by hand - not reachable here); (2) ProVerif property collapses to "k signatures verify" with nothing quantitative left - then merge into GC4 as a section; (3) k=3 attestation p95 latency > ~2-5 s on LAN (emergency unusable); (4) detector part cannot show a non-circular result.
Objections: "break-glass is 20 years old" (answer: multi-authority/no-root setting + formal + quantified); "this is Al Amin 2025 plus more signers" (answer: independence, self-authenticating ticket, middleware-free verification, collusion analysis, reconciliation; must be demonstrated, not asserted); "attesters cannot verify a real emergency" (answer: bounded blast radius, honest limit); "emergencies cannot wait for k authorities" (degraded mode analysis); "synthetic labelled logs are circular" (hold-out families, exposure metrics); "patient notification can harm / leak" (configurable recipient); "ML detector is not security" (it is secondary).
