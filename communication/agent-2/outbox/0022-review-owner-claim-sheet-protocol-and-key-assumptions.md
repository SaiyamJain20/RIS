## 2. The single trusted witness (repaired-e only; no counterpart in the paper)
4. **Existence and scope.** One explicitly trusted witness signs StateCheckpoint, AccessDecision and GrantReceipt; paper profiles have none (the witness operations answer WRONG_PROFILE there). Suggestion: extension result (REPAIR_SUPPORTED where agent-3's cases measure it); the paper-profile behaviour is paper-supported by construction. Evidence: `tests/test_witness*.py`, `tests/test_e2_repaired_e.py` (16 catalog cases), `tests/test_paper_profile.py`, A3-0030. Limits: ONE witness, so no quorum, no availability and no equivocation resistance (that is F); honest-witness assumption; the safe-ledger assumption (the latest block is treated as final; a block hash is an observation, not a light-client proof).
5. **Decisions are recomputed, never relayed.** The witness reads PBC state at the checkpoint block and its timestamp, evaluates the DBC policy (`ACSC.evaluate`) and signs ALLOW only if both hold; otherwise a signed DENY (empty records when credentials lapsed by the DBC clock); a consumed nonce is refused (REPLAY) before any signature. Suggestion: tested reconstruction result with two hardening findings (the witness had been policy-blind, A1-0017; it could sign an ALLOW the DBC would revert). Evidence: `tests/test_witness_policy.py`, `test_witness_hardening.py`, mutation 65/73. Limit: defence against a faulty witness is partial and only through the ledger's own checks: a forged ALLOW for a policy-denied subject reverts POLICY_DENIED (tested), a deviating receipt is rejected by the PSC binding (measured with the TEST-ONLY tool); nothing detects a witness that signs a consistent but false state.
6. **Freshness constants.** Request and decision TTL 30 s, checkpoint TTL 60 s, `now >= expiresAt` rejects, on the DBC clock; a decision is invalidated by any newer accepted checkpoint (sequence-equality barrier). Suggestion: ambiguity resolved by explicit choice (the paper gives no numbers), with the measured consequence: after a PBC revocation a decision collected before the DBC accepts the new checkpoint stayed consumable until its own expiry (+29 s accepted, +30 s EXPIRED), so the stale window is bounded by the decision TTL, not the checkpoint TTL. Evidence: A2-0009 section 2 (the measurement, taken on my machine) and `evidence/e2-repaired-e-observed.txt` for the catalog observations; I have not re-measured the window since. Limits: second-level figures on local Anvil clocks are indicative; PBC/DBC clock skew is not corrected.
7. **A receipt is grant evidence, never delivery evidence.** `attest_grant` signs a GrantReceipt only for an allowed grant read from the DBC; the PSC records the outcome. Suggestion: extension result. Evidence: `tests/test_supplement_controls.py` (receipt binding on repaired-e vs the mutant), E1 run. Limit: nothing here proves a user received anything.
